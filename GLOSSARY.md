# Workspace Tab History

Tab history identifies recently used tabs within each Herdr workspace. Each workspace's history is independent of other workspaces and sessions.

## Language

**Current tab**:
The active tab in a workspace.

**Last tab**:
The most recently used surviving tab retained in a workspace's history other than its current tab.
_Avoid_: Previous workspace, adjacent tab

**Workspace tab history**:
An ordered list of a workspace's recently used distinct surviving tabs, including its current tab. A newly tracked workspace has no last tab; closing the workspace removes its history.
